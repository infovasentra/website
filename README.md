## 1. Project Overview & Capabilities

VASENTRA operates as the premier bilateral gateway between India and China across 5 primary service pillars:
1. **Mandarin Chinese Language Courses:**
   - **Kids (Ages 5–15):** Little Pandas (5–7), Young Explorers (8–11), Junior Achievers (12–15) via playful storytelling, games, YCT exams.
   - **Business Professionals:** Traders, importers, manufacturers (Yiwu, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Canton Fair preparation).
   - **Students & HSK Aspirants:** Comprehensive preparation for HSK 1 to HSK 6.
   - **General Mandarin:** Fast-track conversational to native-level fluency.
2. **Study in China & Scholarships:**
   - Admissions guidance for Bachelor's, Master's, PhD, MBBS, Engineering, and Language programs.
   - Scholarship applications: Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC), Provincial, University, and International Chinese Language Teachers (CIS) scholarships.
   - 7-Step end-to-end admission, translation, attestation, visa JW202, and pre-departure briefing.
3. **Interpretation Services:**
   - **In India:** Supporting Chinese delegations, factory technicians, business negotiations, and legal appointments.
   - **In China:** On-ground bilingual interpreters for Canton Fair, factory audits, and supplier disputes.
4. **Document Translation:**
   - High-accuracy translation across **Chinese ↔ English ↔ Hindi**.
   - Certified translation for legal contracts, engineering manuals, degree certificates, and commercial catalogs.
5. **Corporate Outsourcing:**
   - On-demand bilingual staffing, dedicated contract interpreters, and long-term factory support.
6. **Free Resources:**
   - Interactive Word of the Day with native pronunciation audio.
   - Downloadable guides ("100 Essential Business Phrases", "Study in China Scholarship Roadmap").
