## 2. Flagship Program: Mandarin Language Course (HSK 1 to HSK 6)

### Key Metrics:
- **6 Progressive Levels** (HSK 1 to HSK 6)
- **5,000 Total Words** at HSK 6
- **390 Total Hours** of structured learning
- **1 Billion+** Mandarin speakers worldwide

### Curriculum Breakdown:
1. **HSK 1 (Beginner | 150 Words | 40 Hours):**
   - Pinyin, initials, finals, and the 4 tones.
   - Greetings, names, nationality, and family.
   - Numbers, dates, days, and telling time.
   - Daily routine, food, likes, and dislikes.
   - Directions and location words.
2. **HSK 2 (Elementary | 300 Words | 50 Hours):**
   - Weather, health, and shopping.
   - Transport, travel, and booking.
   - Hobbies, invitations, and ability.
   - Ongoing and completed actions.
   - Short notes of 5 to 6 sentences.
3. **HSK 3 (Pre-Intermediate | 600 Words | 60 Hours):**
   - Describing people, places, and experiences.
   - Past experience and future plans.
   - Travel planning and emergencies.
   - Complements and linking conjunctions.
   - Reading passages of ~300 words.
4. **HSK 4 (Intermediate | 1,200 Words | 70 Hours):**
   - Careers and workplace communication.
   - Opinions, advice, and complaints.
   - Formal and informal registers.
   - Set expressions (*Chengyu*) introduced.
   - 3-minute oral presentations.
5. **HSK 5 (Pre-Advanced | 2,500 Words | 80 Hours):**
   - Media and news vocabulary.
   - Debate and persuasive speaking.
   - Reports, summaries, and formal letters.
   - Idioms and literary expressions.
   - Introduction to Chinese literature and cinema.
6. **HSK 6 (Advanced | 5,000 Words | 90 Hours):**
   - Authentic TV, radio, and news broadcasts.
   - Abstract and specialized technical topics.
   - Summarizing long texts and spoken recordings.
   - Business negotiations, interviews, and executive presentations.
   - Full HSK 6 mock tests and scoring.

## 5. Technical Architecture & Design Standards

- **Theme:** Apple-Style Glassy UI
  - Background: Crisp, light, ultra-clean warm canvas (`#F8FAFC` to `#FAF7F2`)
  - Glass Panels: Frosted acrylic `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`
  - Specular Borders: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` + subtle inset lighting
  - Elevation: Soft diffuse ambient shadows (`0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)`)
- **Interactive Capabilities:**
  - **Pronunciation Soundboard:** Browser Speech Synthesis (`zh-CN` voice) & Web Audio tones for `你好`, `谢谢`, and `欢迎`.
  - **Dynamic HSK Matrix:** Instant level switcher without page reloads or long scroll fatigue.
  - **Persona Selector:** Dynamic highlighting tailored for Students, Traders, and Professionals.
  - **Sticky Mobile Dock:** Floating mobile conversion dock with WhatsApp and Call actions.
  - **Responsive & Zero-Overflow:** 100% fluid grid with `clamp()` typography and touch-friendly (48px+) tap targets.
