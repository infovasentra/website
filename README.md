## 2. Interactive Learning Studio & Features

- **Vocabulary Vault:** Filterable HSK 1–3 and Business Chinese words with native audio pronunciation (`zh-CN` Web Speech Synthesis).
- **Sentence Formation Studio:** Gamified block-assembly engine verifying proper Mandarin syntax with grammar rules and audio playback.
- **Word of the Day Widget:** Daily rotating card with character, pinyin, meaning, example sentence, and audio.
- **Study in China Portal:** End-to-end guidance for university admissions, CSC government scholarships, JW202 visa, and 7-step timeline.
- **Mobile Sticky Conversion Dock:** Quick 1-tap `[Call]`, `[WhatsApp]`, and `[Book Free Demo]`.
